Just 5 Letters

How to Play

Guess the hidden 5-letter word. You have 6 tries.

Tap letters on the keyboard, then tap Enter. Tap Delete to fix a letter.

After each guess, the tiles show how close you are:

W
Green ✓ — right letter, right spot.
O
Gold ↔ — in the word, but in a different spot.
S
Gray — not in the word.

Stuck? Tap Hint for one free letter. There's no timer, so take your time. A new word arrives every day at midnight.
